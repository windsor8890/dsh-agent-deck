# Runtime soak — 2026-11-06

Environment: DSH Web GUI `http://127.0.0.1:7246`, plugin enabled via **插件 → 已安装 → 监控室**,
1280×800, light theme, plugin panel cropped from `x=280`.

## Passed

- **9 concurrent sessions**: `AGENTS 9`, 8 desks occupied, `STANDBY +1` tray,
  wall `LIVE FEED +1`. Baselines:
  [`office-light.png`](baselines/office-light.png),
  [`seat-overflow-light.png`](baselines/seat-overflow-light.png).
- **Focus / ANALYZE**: clicking a TUI card enters ANALYZE; the six widgets render and
  `× RELEASE` returns to the office. Baseline:
  [`analyze-light.png`](baselines/analyze-light.png).
- **PREFS**: the panel opens with all four sections; toggling `DESK ACTIONS`
  OFF/ON was verified against the office labels and persisted. Baseline:
  [`prefs-light.png`](baselines/prefs-light.png).
- **Social visits**: with 9 sessions, `CHATTING` / `VISITING` labels and a
  `DELEGATE` bubble were observed.
- **Idle release**: after the helper turns ended and the 3-minute retention
  elapsed, the helper seats dropped from 8/8 to 2/8 and the `STANDBY +1` tray
  disappeared. This confirms seat release, but did not isolate the
  standby→freed-seat hand-off (all helpers ended together).
- **Standby hand-off**: a second run used 7 short helpers (45 s) and one long
  standby helper (300 s), started last. At 13:51 the long helper was in the
  `STANDBY +1` tray while all 8 desks were occupied; by 13:54:55 the short
  sessions had been retained/released and the long helper was seated at a
  desk (`AGENTS 2`, standby tray gone). Hand-off PASS.
- **Host cost proxy**: 14 `DeepSeek Harness` processes, 18 logical cores,
  1.47 CPU-seconds over 10 s ≈ **0.8% host CPU** during the 9-agent light
  office (not a frame-rate measurement).
- **Static checks**: `npm test` 13/13; client 456,310 B, gzip 216,969 B,
  assets 155,686 B.
